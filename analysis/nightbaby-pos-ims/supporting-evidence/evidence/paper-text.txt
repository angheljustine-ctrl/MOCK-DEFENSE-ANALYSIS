1. INTRODUCTION 
The Nightbaby Point -of-Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System is a 
proposed web -based retail management system designed for a business engaged in 
fashion, clothing, and perfume products. The system focuses on two main functions: 
processing sales transactions and managing inventory records. A POS system records 
and processes transactions when products are sold, while inventory management 
involves monitoring the quantity and movement of products available for sale. IBM 
explains that inventory management h elps businesses maintain visibility of their 
products, determine when stock should be replenished, and maintain appropriate 
inventory levels (IBM, n.d.). These functions are closely connected because each 
completed sale affects the remaining quantity of products in inventory. 
POS technology has developed from traditional cash registers into 
computerized systems that can record detailed transaction information and support 
other retail operations. IBM explains that early POS systems were designed to automate 
transaction recording , identify products, determine prices, and generate sales 
information. The development of centralized POS systems also allowed sales 
information to support inventory and restocking decisions (IBM, n.d.). This shows that 
POS systems can serve not only as a means of recording sales but also as a source of 
information for managing products. 
The importance of organized sales and inventory processes is also evident in the 
Philippine retail sector. The Philippine Statistics Authority reported that the 2022 
Annual Survey of Philippine Business and Industry recorded 125,257 formal -sector 
establishments engaged in wholesale and retail trade and related activities. Of these, 
34,280 establishments belonged to the industry group for retail sale of other goods in 
specialized stores (Philippine Statistics Authority [PSA], 2025). These figures 
demonstrate the significant presence of retail activities in the country's formally 
recorded business sector.

Accurate inventory records are important because they should correspond with 
the actual quantity of products available. IBM notes that accurate inventory information 
helps businesses determine when and how much stock should be ordered, while 
insufficient o r excessive inventory can create operational problems (IBM, n.d.). 
Research by DeHoratius and Raman (2008) also found inventory record inaccuracies 
in a study involving nearly 370,000 inventory records from 37 stores. Although the 
study involved only one r etailer and should not be generalized to all businesses, it 
provides evidence that differences between recorded and physical inventory can occur. 
The proposed Nightbaby system connects POS and inventory functions within 
one platform. Oracle's retail documentation explains that POS transactions can be used 
to update inventory positions based on sales and other transactions (Oracle, n.d.). In the 
proposed system, the POS component is intended to record product orders and sales, 
including products, quantities, prices, payment information, and transaction records. 
The inventory component is intended to maintain product information and stock 
quantities. Connecting these functions allows sales information and inventory records 
to be managed together in an organized manner. 
JavaScript is included in the web -system technology requirement because it 
supports interactive behavior on web pages. The Mozilla Developer Network (MDN) 
explains that JavaScript can respond to user actions, modify webpage content, and 
provide client -side form validation (MDN Web Docs, n.d.). These capabilities are 
applicable to product selection, transaction forms, quantity fields, search functions, 
buttons, and inventory forms. JavaScript can therefore provide immediate feedback and 
interaction while users perform sales and inventory-related tasks. 
Overall, the Nightbaby Point -of-Sale and Inventory Management System is 
proposed as a centralized web-based system that connects product sales and inventory 
management. The system is intended to organize product orders and sales, maintain 
transaction records, and monitor product quantities. By connecting these functions, the 
system aims to support more organized and accurate sales and inventory records. 
JavaScript will support the interactive features of the web interface, including user 
input, dynamic behavior, and form validation.

2. PROJECT OVERVIEW 
The Point-of-Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System (IMS) provides a 
centralized platform for managing the main retail operations of the business. The POS 
focuses on sales transactions and customer service activities, while the IMS focuses on 
product and inventory management. The system also provides supporting functions for 
transaction history, reports, discounts, user management, and Backup and Restore . A 
Product Display Website is also provided for customers to browse products and 
services. 
2.1 System Name 
Nightbaby Point-of-Sale and Inventory Management System 
2.2 System Description 
The system combines Point-of-Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System 
(IMS) functions into one platform. The POS manages sales transactions, product 
selection, and discounts, while the IMS manages product information, inventory 
records, product searching , and stock adjustments. The system has three main user 
roles: Administrator, Cashier, and Super Admin. It also provides transaction history, 
reports, discount management, user management, Backup and Restore, and a Product 
Display Website for customers to browse products and services. 
. 
2.3 Problem Statement 
The manual retail processes of Nightbaby result in several operational 
problems. First, sales transactions are processed manually, which slows down service 
and increases the risk of computation errors in subtotal, total, and change. Similarly, 
some micro, small, and medium enterprises still rely on manual recording methods, 
which create risks of human error, inefficiency, and poor decision -making capacity 
(Yuniarto et al., 2025). Second, inventory records for clothing  and perfumes are not 
consistently updated, making it difficult to track actual stock levels or notice low-stock 
and expiring perfume items in time. Third, because sales and inventory records are kept 
separately, preparing business reports takes additional time and is prone to 
inconsistencies. Finally, there is no organized way to manage different levels of user 
access or to protect business data from loss.

2.4 Proposed Solution 
To address these problems, the proposed system provides an integrated Point -
of-Sale (POS) and Inventory Management System (IMS). The POS module automates 
the sales process by computing the subtotal, total, and change automatically, reducing 
manual errors a nd speeding up transactions. The IMS module centralizes inventory 
records and provides low-stock and perfume-expiry alerts, allowing stock levels to be 
monitored more accurately. The Reports module consolidates sales and inventory data 
with filtering and PDF export, reducing the time needed to prepare business reports. 
Lastly, role-based access for the Administrator, Cashier, and Super Admin, together 
with Backup and Restore functionality, provides organized and secure management of 
the system and its data.

5. PROJECT LIMITATIONS 
The system is primarily designed for the current operations of Nightbaby ™ 
Incorporated and may require modifications if used by other businesses. The system 
does not include other payment methods such as debit cards and bank accounts, and 
only supports e-wallets and cash. It also does not include advanced business analytics, 
automated supplier management, or direct shipping and delivery management . 
Nationwide shipping may still be offered by the business through its regular processes, 
but shipping and delivery transactions are not managed directly within the system. The 
accuracy of inventory records also depends on the information entered by authorized 
users. Additionally, the system is limited to browser’s “localStorage” that can only hold 
~5MB Shared across tabs and windows (Tomita, 2025). Due to this, the system is not 
capable of handling online controls and it is self-contained within its own system, and 
the system is limited to desktop set up only.

11. CONCLUSION 
The Nightbaby Point -of-Sale and Inventory Management System was 
developed to address the operational challenges brought about by Nightbaby's manual 
retail processes. The completed system integrates a Point-of-Sale (POS) module and an 
Inventory Management S ystem (IMS) into a single platform, allowing sales 
transactions, inventory records and business reports to be managed in one place. Role-
based access for the Administrator, Cashier, and Super Admin was implemented to 
organize system responsibilities, while Backup and Restore functionality was added to 
support data protection. 
Throughout development, the project's specific objectives were achieved: sales 
transactions are now automated, with subtotal, total, and change computed 
automatically; inventory records are centralized with low -stock and perfume -expiry 
alerts; and consolidated sales and inventory reports with filtering and PDF export are 
available for faster business reporting. 
JavaScript played a central role in making the system interactive and responsive. 
It was used to validate user input on forms such as login and account settings, to 
dynamically update the shopping cart and totals in real time, to handle role -based 
interface changes between the Administrator, Cashier, and Super Admin views, and to 
manage data persistence through the browser's localStorage, since the system operates 
fully local without a database or web server. 
Developing this system allowed the team to apply concepts learned in Web 
System Technologies with JavaScript to a practical, real -world retail scenario, 
strengthening skills in front -end scripting, form validation, user interface design, and 
role-based access control. For future improvements, the system may be enhanced by 
migrating from localStorage to a proper database for multi-device access, adding online 
payment processing.
