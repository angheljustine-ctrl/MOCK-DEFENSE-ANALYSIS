P26: ABSTRACT
P27: RevCast is a die-cast toy company in Valenzuela, Metro Manila that produces and sells 1:64 scale model cars. As the company grows, its employee records, attendance, leave and salary computations need to be handled accurately and securely. This study presents the RevCast Employee Management and Payroll System, a web-based system that automates these tasks. Employees can clock in and out, file leave requests and view or print their payslips. The system uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the interface and PHP on an XAMPP server for sign-in, role checks and data storage. Passwords are hashed, access is limited by role, and every request is checked on the server. The system gives RevCast a faster, more accurate and more secure way to pay its employees.
P28: Keywords: payroll, employee management, attendance, payslip, role-based access

P68: INTRODUCTION
P69: 1.1 Background of the Study
P70: Payroll is one of the most important tasks of any company. Employees depend on it to receive the correct salary on time, and the company depends on it to keep accurate records and follow government requirements. When payroll is done by hand or with scattered spreadsheets, mistakes in attendance counting, contribution rates and deductions are common, and the work takes more time as the number of employees grows.
P71: RevCast is a die-cast toy company established in 2026 in Valenzuela, Metro Manila. It designs, produces and sells 1:64 scale model cars for children, hobbyists and collectors, beginning with the BURNSTREAK-GT "Dudge Dimunyu". With staff working in production, quality inspection, marketing, sales, logistics and administration, the company needs a dependable way to record attendance and compute pay. This study developed a web-based Employee Management and Payroll System for RevCast to meet that need.
P72: 1.2 Statement of the Problem
P73: Without a system, RevCast faces the following problems:
P74: 1.  Employee records and attendance are kept in separate files, which makes them hard to update and verify.
P75: 2.  Salary, government contributions, tax and absences are computed manually, which takes time and can produce errors.
P76: 3.  Employees have no easy way to check their attendance, leave status and payslips.
P77: 4.  Payroll data is private, but paper and shared files are easy to read, copy or change by unauthorized people.
P78: 1.3 Significance of the Study
P79: The study benefits the following groups. The company owner and administrators gain faster and more accurate payroll and better control of records. The HR and payroll officer saves time because attendance, leave and salary computation are connected in one system. Employees can see their own attendance and payslips at any time. Future researchers can use this study as a reference for developing a small-business payroll system.
P80: PROJECT OVERVIEW
P81: 2.1 About the System
P82: The RevCast Employee Management and Payroll System is a website that runs on a local server using XAMPP. Users sign in with an email and password. After signing in, administrators and HR officers see the management pages, while employees see only their own pages.
P83: 2.2 How the System Works
P84: Employees record their time through clock in and clock out. They may also file leave requests, which the administrator approves or rejects. At the end of the month, the administrator or HR officer selects the month and runs payroll. The system reads each employee's salary, attendance and approved leave, computes the pay and saves a payslip for each employee. Employees then open their payslip, view it and print it. 
P86: PROJECT GOAL AND OBJECTIVES
P87: 3.1 General Objective
P88: To develop a secure and easy-to-use web-based system that manages employee information and produces accurate payroll and payslips for RevCast.
P89: 3.2 Specific Objectives
P90: The system aims to:
P91: 1.  Store employee and department records in one place.
P92: 2.  Record daily attendance through clock in and clock out.
P93: 3.  Allow employees to file leave requests and allow the administrator to approve or reject them.
P94: 4.  Compute monthly payroll automatically, including allowance, SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG, withholding tax and absences.
P95: 5.  Generate payslips that employees can view and print.
P96: 6.  Control access by role (admin, HR and employee) and require admin approval for new accounts.
P97: 7.  Protect the data from unauthorized access and changes.
P98: PROJECT SCOPE
P99: 4.1 Coverage
P100: Account creation with admin approval, login, logout and password reset requests
P101: Employee records and departments
P102: Daily attendance and leave management
P103: Monthly payroll computation and payslip generation
P104: Reports with CSV export
P105: Role-based access for admin, HR and employee
P106: System settings such as company name, allowance and work start time
P107: 4.2 Exclusions
P108: The system does not include bank or online salary transfer, biometric attendance devices, filing of reports with government agencies, or a mobile application.
P109: PROJECT LIMITATIONS
P110: The system runs on a local XAMPP server. Other computers on the same network can open it, but it is not hosted on the internet.
P111: Data is stored in a protected file on the server instead of a full database. This is suitable for a small company but not for a very large one.
P112: The system cannot send emails. When a user forgets a password, the admin sets a temporary password.
P113: Payroll uses simplified rates for SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG and withholding tax. The rates should be checked against the current official tables before real use.
P114: Attendance is recorded through the browser and does not use fingerprint or face recognition.
P115: Payroll is computed monthly. Overtime, bonuses, loans and holiday pay are not yet included.

P143: SYSTEM FEATURES
P144: 7.1 Authentication
P145: Login with email and password, with an icon to show or hide the password
P146: Create account, which stays "Pending" until the admin approves it
P147: Forgot password request, answered by the admin with a temporary password
P148: Automatic logout after 30 minutes of inactivity
P149: Lockout for 10 minutes after 5 wrong password attempts
P150: 7.2 Employee, Attendance and Leave Management
P151: Admin and HR can add, edit, search and delete employees, manage departments, and view daily attendance marked as on time, late or absent. Employees clock in and out from their dashboard. Leave requests are approved or rejected by the admin, and approved leave is not counted as an absence in payroll.
P152: 7.3 Payroll Computation
P153: Payroll is computed per employee for the selected month using the rules in Table 2.
P154: Table 2. Payroll computation rules
P155: Item
P156: Rule
P157: Gross pay
P158: Basic salary + monthly allowance
P159: SSS
P160: 4.5% of basic salary, maximum ₱1,350
P161: PhilHealth
P162: 2.5% of basic salary, maximum ₱2,500
P163: Pag-IBIG
P164: 2% of basic salary, maximum ₱200
P165: Absences
P166: (Basic salary ÷ working days in the month) × days absent without approved leave
P167: Taxable income
P168: Gross pay − absences − SSS − PhilHealth − Pag-IBIG
P169: Withholding tax
P170: 15% of taxable income above ₱20,833
P171: Net pay
P172: Gross pay − absences − SSS − PhilHealth − Pag-IBIG − withholding tax
P174: Example: an employee with a basic salary of ₱28,000, an allowance of ₱1,500 and no absences has a gross pay of ₱29,500. The deductions are SSS ₱1,260.00, PhilHealth ₱700.00 and Pag-IBIG ₱200.00. Taxable income is ₱27,340.00, so the withholding tax is ₱976.05 and the net pay is ₱26,363.95.
P175: 7.4 Payslip
P176: Each payslip shows the company header, the employee details, earnings, deductions and a highlighted net pay. Admin and HR can open any payslip. Employees can open only their own current payslip and past payslips, and every payslip can be printed.
P177: 7.5 Reports and Settings
P178: Reports show payroll totals per month, headcount and salary totals per department, and a leave summary. Payroll and report data can be exported as CSV files. The admin can change the company name, address, allowance and work start time.

P200: SYSTEM DESIGN
P201: 9.1 System Architecture
P202: The system has two main parts. The front end, written in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, shows the pages in the browser. The back end, api.php, runs on the server and handles sign-in, role checks and saving of data. The browser never reads the data file directly. 
P205: 9.2 System Flowchart
P206: A user must enter a valid email and password and must have an account approved by the admin. The system then checks the role and opens the admin and HR pages or the employee pages.

P274: CONCLUSION
P275: 11.1 Summary
P276: The RevCast Employee Management and Payroll System replaces manual payroll with a single organized website. It stores employee records, records attendance, handles leave, computes payroll and produces payslips that employees can view and print. Role-based access, hashed passwords and server-side checks help keep the information safe.
P277: 11.2 Recommendations
P278: Upgrade the data storage to a MySQL database as the number of employees grows.
P279: Add email notifications for approvals, password resets and payslip release.
P280: Add overtime, holiday pay, bonuses and loan deductions to the payroll computation.
P281: Use biometric or QR-based attendance devices.
P282: Host the system online with HTTPS so employees can access it from outside the office.
P283: Review the contribution and tax rates every year against the official tables.
