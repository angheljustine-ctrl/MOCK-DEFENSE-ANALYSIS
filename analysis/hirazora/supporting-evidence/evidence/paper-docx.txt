INTRODUCTION 
HIRAZORA Studio Co. is a fashion and apparel business that needs a more organized way to manage products, monitor stock, handle sales transactions, and keep track of users who access the system. The proposed HIRAZORA Studio Co. Inventory and Point of Sale System is a web-based application designed to bring these activities into one centralized system. Instead of relying heavily on separate records or manual checking, the system is intended to make everyday store operations more organized, easier to monitor, and less time-consuming.
The system focuses on two connected areas: inventory management and point of sale. Inventory management covers products, categories, stock levels, stock movement, and low-stock monitoring. The point-of-sale component is intended to support product selection, cart management, payment entry, transaction totals, receipts, and automatic inventory deductions after a completed sale. A user management module is also included so that authorized staff can have accounts and access can be controlled according to their assigned role.
JavaScript plays an important part in making the web pages interactive. It is used for client-side validation, opening and closing interfaces, searching and filtering records, updating visible values, handling cart interactions, showing or hiding passwords, and responding immediately to user actions without requiring every small interaction to reload the page. The current development stage is focused on the frontend experience using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; database-backed PHP and MySQL integration can be connected after the major interface modules have been completed and tested.
The primary users of the system are authorized HIRAZORA personnel who are involved in administration, inventory control, sales, and reporting. Access to administrative functions is expected to be limited to users with appropriate permissions.






PROJECT OVERVIEW
2.1 System Name 
HIRAZORA Studio Co. Inventory and Point of Sale System
2.2 System Description 
The proposed system is a browser-based business application for HIRAZORA Studio Co. It combines user management, product organization, inventory monitoring, point-of-sale transactions, sales records, and reporting in one interface. The system is designed around a dashboard-style environment where users can move between modules through a consistent navigation menu.
The application is planned to use a clear role-based structure. Administrators can manage user accounts and system access, while operational users can work with the functions assigned to their role. The interface follows the HIRAZORA visual identity using a dark navy and black foundation, electric-blue accents, the official logo, and a consistent angular visual treatment. The current prototype is being developed first as a frontend so that the layout and user experience can be reviewed before database and server-side functions are connected.
2.3 Problem Statement
Store operations can become difficult to manage when product information, stock records, sales details, and user access are handled separately. Manual recording can make it harder to identify low-stock items, review previous transactions, maintain accurate product information, and determine who is authorized to use administrative functions. These difficulties can also lead to duplicated work, delayed updates, and avoidable recording mistakes.
The project therefore addresses the need for a centralized web system that can organize product and inventory information, support faster sales processing, provide useful records and reports, and give the business a clearer method of controlling user access.
2.4 Propose Solution 
The proposed solution is an integrated Inventory and Point of Sale System where the main store processes are connected through a single web application. Product records can be organized by category, inventory quantities can be monitored, and sales transactions can be processed through the POS interface. User accounts and roles can also be managed from a dedicated module. By linking these modules, the system is intended to reduce repetitive manual work and provide more consistent information across daily operations.

PROJECT LIMITATIONS
Like most web-based information systems, the proposed system has certain limitations that define the boundaries of its use and implementation. These limitations are considered during development to ensure that the system remains practical, manageable, and appropriate for its intended users:
The system is designed primarily for use through a modern web browser and may depend on the capabilities and compatibility of the device being used.
A dedicated native mobile application is outside the scope of the project unless it is specifically developed as a separate component.
System performance may vary depending on the available computer resources, browser performance, network condition, and overall environment in which the system is operated.
Some functions may require compatible hardware or additional peripherals, such as barcode scanners, receipt printers, or other point-of-sale devices, which may need separate setup and configuration.
Features that depend on external services, internet access, email delivery, or other third-party resources may be affected when those services are unavailable or incorrectly configured.
Access to administrative and restricted functions is limited to authorized users and is subject to the access rules established by the system.
The system depends on the accuracy and completeness of the information entered and maintained by its users. Incorrect, incomplete, or outdated data may affect the accuracy of system results and reports.
The system will only be as accurate as the information entered and maintained by authorized users.

Reports — Provides organized summaries of sales and inventory information for monitoring, review, and decision support.
